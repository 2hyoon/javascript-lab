Modal Component

A modal dialog takes over the page: while it is open, nothing behind it can be clicked, tabbed to, or read by a screen reader. That is what separates it from a panel that merely sits on top of the page, and it is the part that is easy to get wrong by hand.

This one is a native <dialog> opened with showModal(), so the browser supplies most of the hard parts: Esc closes it, the rest of the page goes inert, and focus returns to this button afterwards. Only the click outside the panel and the Tab wrap are scripted.

You are inside the dialog

Press Tab on the last button and focus comes straight back to the close button above; Shift + Tab on that one sends it to the last. The cycle never leaves this dialog, and never reaches the page behind.

Only half of that is the browser's doing. showModal() makes the rest of the page inert, which is what keeps the content behind out of reach — but it lets Tab pass through the browser's own toolbar on the way round. The wrap is scripted.
